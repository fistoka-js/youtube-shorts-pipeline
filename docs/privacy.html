Privacy Policy

YouTube Pipeline is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It has no other users.

What the app accesses

The app requests the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) for the owner's own Google account. It uses this permission only to upload videos, captions, and thumbnails to that channel.

What is stored

The authorization token Google issues is stored on the owner's computer and in the owner's private, encrypted GitHub Actions secrets, so scheduled uploads can run. The app does not collect, store, or process personal data about any other person.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, or disclose any Google user data to third parties.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue at github.com/fistoka-js/youtube-shorts-pipeline/issues.
